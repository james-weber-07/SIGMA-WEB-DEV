SPACE MERCURY

ABOUT MERCURY

Mercury is the smallest and innermost planet in the Solar System. It is a rocky planet with a solid, cratered surface, similar to the Moon.

SPACE VENUS

ABOUT VENUS

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is Earth's closest planetary neighbor. It is similar in size and composition to Earth, but has a thick, toxic atmosphere that traps heat.

SPACE EARTH

ABOUT EARTH

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. About 29.2% of Earth's surface is land, with the remaining 70.8% covered with water.

SPACE MARS

ABOUT MARS

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet in the Solar System, after Mercury. It is often referred to as the "Red Planet" due to its reddish appearance, which is caused by iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

SPACE JUPITER

ABOUT JUPITER

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a thick atmosphere, known for its Great Red Spot, a giant storm larger than Earth.

SPACE SATURN

ABOUT SATURN

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the Solar System, after Jupiter. It is best known for its extensive ring system, which is made up of ice and rock particles.

SPACE URANUS

ABOUT URANUS

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is unique for its tilted axis, which causes extreme seasonal variations. It is an ice giant with a cold atmosphere and faint rings.

SPACE NEPTUNE

ABOUT NEPTUNE

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun in the Solar System. It is known for its strong winds and dark blue color, which is due to the presence of methane in its atmosphere.
